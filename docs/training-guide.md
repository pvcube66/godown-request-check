# Quick guide for telecallers and administrators

## Signing in
Use your own email and password. After five wrong tries the login locks for 15 minutes. You see only the districts you were given.

## Your day
1. **Unusual requests** (first page): hostels that asked for much more than usual. Open a hostel's tile to see the charts.
   - It is really wrong: press **Issue (reject this indent)** and write what is wrong. This only records it here.
     Then go to the government portal and **reject the indent there yourself**. This system can only read the portal.
   - It is fine: press **Fine**.
2. **Issues**: after you reject on the portal, the hostel has to send a new indent. This list shows who is still to send one.
   Red "overdue" means the wait has passed the limit (2 days unless an administrator changed it). Call the hostel (tap the number on its page) and write a **remark** with what they said.
3. **Order tracking**: every order with its stage and how long it has been there. Orange = a week or more, red = two weeks or more. Click a request ID for the full story.
4. **Dashboards**: counts by stage, district or godown. Click any number to see those orders. "Download Excel" on each.
5. **Supplier performance**: which suppliers are meeting demand, and which hostels are being served short.
6. **Search** (top of every page): hostel name, part of a name, hostel code, or request ID.

## The rules for counting days
Days run only while an order is Pending, DC Generated, In-Transit or in Variation. When it is Acknowledged the count stops and the date is shown in green. Rejected and Cancelled are final.

## Spotting something the system missed
Open the order and use **Flag this order** with a reason. It joins Issues.

## WhatsApp reminders (when switched on)
Hostels get a reminder when a delivery has been In-Transit for 3 days without being acknowledged, then every 2 days, up to 4. It stops when the order is acknowledged. If a hostel says "stop", record it on the hostel page. You can see every reminder, its ticks and the replies on the order page.

## Administrators only
- **Users**: add people, choose their districts, reset passwords, turn logins off.
- **Master data**: district-to-godown-to-supplier mapping, item units, contacts. Re-assigning a district keeps the history.
- **Godowns**: the portal logins we read from. A red notice on the first page means one is failing.
- **WhatsApp**: settings, wording approval, preview of who would be messaged.
- **Full copy**: Excel files with everything, for keeping a year of records.

## If something looks wrong
A red bar on the first page says the hourly check has not worked for over two hours: tell an administrator. Never share your password; the emergency login is for administrators only.
